CarryMate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CarryMate ("we", "us", "our") connects people who need to send items ("senders") with travelers who can carry them ("travelers"), and separately lets users buy, sell, or request items from each other while traveling ("Discover"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: full name, email address, phone number, and a securely hashed password when you register.

Identity verification (KYC): if you choose to verify your identity (required for higher-value deliveries), we collect your Aadhaar number, PAN number, and photos of these documents, plus a driving license number and photo if you register as a traveler using a car or bike.

Photos: your profile photo, package photos, item photos for Discover listings, and handover/delivery confirmation photos you choose to upload.

Location information: pickup and drop city/address for deliveries, and live location during an active delivery so both parties can track progress.

Payment information: we do not store your card or UPI details ourselves. Payments are processed by our payment partner, Razorpay, and we retain only the transaction ID, amount, and status.

Payout information (travelers): UPI ID or bank account details you provide so we can pay you for completed deliveries.

Communications: messages you send through in-app chat with other users, and any reviews or ratings you leave.

Emergency contact information: phone numbers of emergency contacts you choose to provide, used only in safety-related situations.

Device information: a push notification token so we can send you delivery updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and match senders with travelers
  • To verify identity and improve trust and safety on the platform
  • To process payments and payouts
  • To enable communication between matched users
  • To send you notifications about your deliveries, requests, and account
  • To investigate disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms of Service

3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Razorpay — to process payments and payouts securely
  • Supabase — our backend infrastructure provider, which stores app data on our behalf
  • The other party in a transaction — e.g. a sender and traveler matched on a delivery can see each other's name, rating, and contact details necessary to complete that delivery
  • Law enforcement or regulators — only where required by law

4. Data Retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we permanently remove your personally identifying information (name, phone, photos, KYC documents, payout details). However, records of completed transactions (that a delivery or purchase took place, its amount, and date) are retained, since these may be needed by the other party involved, for dispute resolution, or for financial record-keeping — these records no longer identify you personally once your account is deleted.

5. Your Choices

  • Deactivate your account — temporarily hides your profile; logging back in restores it fully
  • Delete your account — permanently removes your personal information and login access; available in Settings, or by contacting us directly if you no longer have the app installed
  • Notification preferences — manageable from within the app

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data in transit, and access controls limiting who can view sensitive information like KYC documents.

7. Children's Privacy

CarryMate is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you through the app.
