1. How your payment is handled

Payments are made in the app and processed by our payment partner, Razorpay. CarryMate holds your payment while the transaction is in progress. The traveler or fulfiller is paid only after delivery or handover has been confirmed. For Discover transactions, the payout is also held for 48 hours after the buyer confirms receipt, so that a problem can still be reported.

2. Delivery bookings: cancelling

  • Before a traveler accepts, or before you pay: you can cancel at any time at no charge. Payment is requested only after a traveler accepts.
  • After you have paid: the cancellation fee depends on how long before the scheduled pickup you cancel. The fee is a percentage of what you paid for the delivery.

  • If you cancel at exactly 72, 24 or 6 hours before pickup, the higher fee applies.
  • The pickup time is the start of the traveler’s departure time slot for that trip.
  • Free cancellation applies only where the table shows no fee. It does not apply to cancellations made closer to the pickup.
  • The fee covers our payment-processing cost, and the rest compensates the traveler for the inconvenience. The app shows the exact fee and your refund before you confirm.
  • If the traveler cancels or the booking fails because of the traveler, you are refunded in full and pay no cancellation fee.
  • After the package is collected: a booking can no longer be cancelled in the app. Use Report a Problem, and our team will review the case.

3. Delivery problems and disputes

If an item is not received, is damaged or is not the item that was agreed, report it through the app as soon as you notice, with a description and, where possible, a photo. We review the evidence from both people and aim to respond within 24 to 48 hours. If a refund is approved, it is paid to your original payment method after the decision. Our decision on a dispute is final, as set out in the Terms.

4. Discover marketplace: cancelling

  • Before payment: either person can cancel at no charge.
  • After payment, before the fulfiller confirms the item is bought: either person can cancel. The buyer is refunded in full.
  • After the fulfiller confirms the item is bought: the buyer can no longer cancel, because the fulfiller has spent their own money on the item. If something is wrong, the buyer can raise a dispute.
  • If the fulfiller cancels after payment, the buyer is refunded in full. A fulfiller who cancels after confirming the item is bought loses trust score points.
  • Handover: the fulfiller marks the item ready, and the buyer confirms receipt with a one-time code. Once receipt is confirmed, the transaction is complete.

5. Discover marketplace: problems and disputes

Once payment is confirmed, either the buyer or the fulfiller can report a problem from the chat. This is possible until handover and for 48 hours after the buyer confirms receipt. Typical reasons are an item not received, an item that is defective or damaged, an item that is not as described, the wrong item, or the other person not responding.

  • While a dispute is open, the payment is on hold and cannot be paid out.
  • The other person can add their side of the story in the chat.
  • We review both statements, any photo evidence and the chat messages.
  • We decide one of the following outcomes: a full refund to the buyer, the payment released to the fulfiller, a split of the payment between them, or the dispute is not upheld.
  • Both people are notified of the outcome. A person found at fault may lose trust score points.
  • Reports that are false or abusive can lead to a trust score deduction and account suspension.

If no dispute is raised within the 48 hours after the buyer confirms receipt, the payment is released to the fulfiller and the transaction is final.

6. How refunds are paid

Our team reviews every refund request and sends it manually, so please allow a short time for the review. A refund shows as "Refunded" in the app only after our payment provider, Razorpay, confirms it. Refunds are returned to the original payment method. After we send a refund, it usually reaches your account within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank and payment method. If a refund fails at the provider, we will start it again and keep you informed.

7. Traveler cancellations and returns

In every case below, if a sender has already paid and the traveler does not complete the delivery, the sender is refunded in full and pays no cancellation fee.

Before pickup

A traveler can cancel a booking before the package is collected. A penalty applies, based on the booking amount, and it is deducted from the traveler\u2019s security deposit or payouts. The traveler also loses trust score points, and repeated cancellations can lead to suspension.

Traveler cancelsPenalty (percentage of the booking amount)
Within 1 hour of accepting the bookingNone
24 hours or more before pickup10%
Less than 24 hours before pickup20%

After pickup

Once a traveler has collected the package, the booking cannot be cancelled. If the traveler cannot continue, they can return the package to the sender. Returning the package is always the right choice, and we make it easy.

  • Returned in time: the traveler has 12 hours from requesting the return to hand the package back. When the sender confirms in the app that the package is back, there is no penalty for the first 2 returns in any 90 days. After that, the penalty is the delivery fee only, whatever the declared value of the package. If the sender confirms the package is back after the 12 hours, the penalty is also the delivery fee.
  • Not returned in time, lost, damaged or not delivered: the declared value of the package is deducted from the traveler\u2019s security deposit and paid to the sender as compensation, up to the amount of the deposit. The traveler\u2019s account may be suspended while we review the case, and we may take further action, including reporting the matter to the police.
  • We review every case manually, and we may reduce or waive a penalty in genuine emergencies.

8. Cancellation by CarryMate

We may cancel a booking or transaction that breaks our Terms, for example one involving a prohibited item. If you have already paid, we will review the case and refund you what is due.
