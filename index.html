CarryMate

A platform connecting people who need to send packages or items with travelers already heading in that direction — and a marketplace to buy, sell, or request items across cities and countries.

What CarryMate Does

CarryMate connects two kinds of independent users:

📦 Senders

People who need a package delivered, matched with a traveler already going that way.

✈️ Travelers

People traveling who can carry an item along their route and earn money for it.

CarryMate also includes Discover, a marketplace where users can buy, sell, or request items to be brought from another city or country.

How It Works

  • A sender posts a delivery request, or a buyer posts an item they want or have for sale
  • A matching traveler or interested user responds
  • Both parties agree, payment is collected securely, and the handover is confirmed with photo and OTP verification at each step
  • Funds are released to the traveler once delivery is confirmed

Payments

All payments on CarryMate are processed securely through Razorpay. CarryMate does not store card or bank details directly.
