Delete Your CarryMate Account

This page lets you request permanent deletion of your CarryMate account and personal data, without needing the app installed. This action cannot be undone.

Before you continue:
• Your login access will be permanently destroyed
• Your name, phone, photos, KYC documents, and payout details will be removed
• Past transaction records are kept (without your personal details) for the other party's history and our financial records
• If you have an active delivery or Discover transaction in progress, deletion will be blocked until it's complete or cancelled
